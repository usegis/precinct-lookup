SOURCES

Precinct map is a June 2026 snapshot from the Statewide Database. Address file
is a 2026 snapshot of parcels published by Tuolumne County. Road centerlines are
a live layer published by Tuolumne County, last edited October 2026. Census
TIGER address ranges and Statewide Database voters by block.
https://github.com/usegis/precinct-lookup#sources
