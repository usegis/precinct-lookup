DATA

This data was developed as a Planning Division tool for general information
purposes only. It is an attempt to create a seamless county-wide layer for land
use planning purposes. The tax parcel boundaries shown do not necessarily
represent a parcel boundary as reflected in a legal description, nor do they
represent legal lots of record. Discrepancies in parcel acreage, shape and
location are common. The information given is not the legal document to be used
in single site determinations. Lake County does not warrant the accuracy of this
information, and no decision involving risk of economic loss or physical injury
should be made in reliance thereon. To accurately locate your parcel boundaries,
contact a licensed surveyor.

SOURCES
